## 🐀 Pigeon & Rat v1.0 (Windows)

**How to play:**
1. Download **PigeonAndRat_Windows.zip** and unzip the whole folder.
2. Double-click **PigeonAndRat.exe**. If SmartScreen warns, choose *More info → Run anyway*.

**Controls:** arrow keys hop · ↑ at the hot dog cart climbs it · P/Esc pause · H help · Enter confirm.

**Goal:** deliver the 5 foods on today's menu to your nest before you lose 3 lives. On WALK the cars stop, but the crowds cross and can step on you. On DONT WALK you jaywalk for a risky combo. Every delivery makes the street busier, and the pigeon attacks more often each time you pick up food.

**Requirements:** Windows 10/11 64-bit and a DirectX 12 GPU. The download is about 390 MB.

A 30 s gameplay demo is attached. Source, README, PROMPT_LOG and ATTRIBUTION are in the repo.
