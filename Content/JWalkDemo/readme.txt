J / WALK - NYC Street Safety Prototype

Open /Game/JWalkDemo/Maps/L_JWalk_NYC and press Play.
WASD: move. Mouse: look. Escape: stop PIE.
Goal: wait for WALK, observe both lanes, then reach the green pad.
20 second cycle: traffic 0-8s, clearance 8-10s, WALK 10-20s.
Four moving taxis, a visibility-obstructing delivery van, collision reset, unsafe-crossing retry, safe-arrival success.
Stylized fictional NYC block; not a geographically accurate reconstruction.
Prototype: simple vehicle paths and collision bounds, English interface, no ambient audio or final art.
Assets isolated under /Game/JWalkDemo; original ThirdPerson level preserved.
